MISSION

Inspire students to explore the aspects of science, technology, engineering, and mathematics through fun and engaging hands-on activities that promote creativity, teamwork, and critical thinking. Students learn by being immersed in the fundamentals of building, experimenting, and solving real-world challenges in a supportive environment.

VISION

Create an environment where students can imagine, design, build, and innovate together while developing the skills needed to complete activities and solve challenges.

Building the Next Generation of Engineers, Innovators, and Problem Solvers.

About Us

WHO WE ARE

My name is Jessica Hines, and I am a Sophomore (Gr.10) at Henry J. Kaiser High School. Growing up, I always had an interest in the world of engineering, especially through exposure to LEGO. This program was created to pass down the same luxury I had to provide local students with opportunities to explore engineering and robotics through hands-on projects.

The goal is to make STEM accessible, engaging, and fun while helping students develop skills that will benefit them throughout their education and future.
